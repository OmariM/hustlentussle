Hustle n' Tussle

Dance Battle Manager

Created by Kenji Tominaga, HNT is a street dance-formatted hustle battle event with a mission to get dancers comfortable beyond social environments, learn to groove to various genres with different partners, and provide consistent opportunities for dancers to grow.

We host Jack & Jill and 7 to Smoke style battles for NewStyle, Classic, and Latin Hustle dancers. Represent your hustle style and step out of your comfort zone!

7 to Smoke Battle Rules

Battle Style

  • Leaders and followers line up separately; the first 2 from each side enter the battle
  • 1 round face to face, 1 min each, 1 song
  • Leader and follower are separately judged
  • Winners earn 1 point per round, accumulated through the season
  • Winning leader and follower stay in the round until they lose
  • Dancers winning 7 rounds straight will be the winner
  • The battle ends in 60 min if no one wins 7 rounds straight

Judging

  • Judges will be guest judges + random judges from the contestants
  • Criteria: Connection, Technique, Musicality, Expression/Spirit
  • Participant judges switch each round

Special Outcomes

  • Tie: no points awarded; the tied dancers face each other again next round
  • No Contest: no points; previous dancers return to the queue and fresh opponents are selected

Expected File Format

A Hustle n' Tussle battle export (a .json file). It records:

  • The battle champions for leads and follows (and how each was decided)
  • Every dancer's final points and placement
  • The full round-by-round history: pairs, winners, judge votes, and songs

Note: the file must be a .json generated by the "Download Battle Data" feature. (Older .xlsx exports are no longer supported.)
